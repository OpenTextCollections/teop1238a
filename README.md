# Open Text Collections #12
## The material culture of the Teop people on Bougainville: Texts on plants, house building, canoes and fishing 

This collection features 32 texts from the Teop language, spoken on the island of Bougainville in Papua New Guinea. 

In this text collection, nine Teop authors and one Teop graphic artist document the use of palms, trees, bamboo and other plants in their material culture. 
 
This repository contain the texts in the CLDF format and the Elan/eaf files on which the collection is based. A human readable version of the book is available under then name "The material culture of the Teop people on Bougainville: Texts on plants, house building, canoes and fishing" at https://zenodo.org/records/14214653.
